## How they were taken
- **Jump grunts:** the user's screen recording `Videos/Screen Recordings/Jump sounds.mp4` (26 Nov 2026), which plays the Freesound previews.
  - Each clip is cut from the moment its preview starts.
  - The take-off grunts (described "while executing a jump") are used.
  - The pack's landing grunts (Land_01 to 04, Jump_05, Jump_06) are in the same recording, unused.
  - The original WAVs need a Freesound login to download, and would be cleaner than the browser preview.
- **Footsteps:** the two Pixabay files in the user's Downloads.
  - Walking steps: the steady first 13 s of "concrete".
  - Running steps: "cellar", cut short so its room noise stays out.
